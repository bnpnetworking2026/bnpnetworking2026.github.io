About

BNP Networking Workshop

The University of Seoul invites you to attend the 4th BNP Networking Workshop, to be held at the University of Seoul (Centennial Memorial Hall) from July 6 (MON) to July 10 (FRI), 2026.

The Bayesian Nonparametric Networking Workshop has been held biennially since its inaugural event in 2022 in Cyprus. The BNP Section launched this series to create smaller, informal gatherings with the following goals:

  • To provide a flexible schedule with dedicated slots for early-career researchers.
  • To establish and strengthen local networks while broadening the appeal of BNP.
  • To foster brainstorming and innovation.

In 2026, the workshop will take place immediately after the ISBA World Meeting in Nagoya, Japan, offering an excellent opportunity to spark engaging discussions and promote deeper networking within the BNP community—particularly valuable for junior researchers.

Call for Contributed Talks & Posters

The Scientific Committee invites the submission of proposals for Contributed Talks and Posters for the 4th BNP Networking Workshop, to be held in Seoul, South Korea, from July 6 to July 10, 2026.

This meeting is part of a workshop series that aims to create an informal event to establish and strengthen local networks and collaborations in Bayesian nonparametrics, with many speaking slots dedicated to early career researchers. There will be foundation lectures given by well-known experts and invited talk sessions featuring latest research.
